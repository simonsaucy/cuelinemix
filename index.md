# Prefade

**In-ear mixes for every singer.**

Prefade saves each performer's in-ear mix and recalls it onto any output, on
any day. Mixes belong to the person, not the channel number — so when a
singer changes microphones their mix follows them, in everyone's ears.

Works with Yamaha DM7, DM3, CL, QL, TF and RIVAGE PM consoles.
Prefade is not affiliated with Yamaha.
