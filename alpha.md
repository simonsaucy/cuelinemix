# Join the alpha

Prefade is being tested with a small number of churches before it goes on
sale. If you run in-ear mixes on a Yamaha desk and you're willing to tell me
what breaks, there's room for you.

That opens a message with the details I need already listed. Send it and
you'll get a TestFlight invitation.

## What you need

- A Yamaha DM7, DM3, CL, QL, TF or RIVAGE PM console. Only the DM7 has been
  tested on real hardware so far — the others are measured at connect rather
  than assumed, but nobody has run them yet.
- A Mac or an iPad.
- The console reachable on the same network.

You do **not** need a console to look around. Prefade ships with a complete
demo DM7 that needs no hardware, and every feature works against it.

## What I'm asking of you

Use it on something that isn't load-bearing first. Recall writes real levels
to a real desk, and while reading from hardware is confirmed, **writing has
not been tested on a real console yet** — an alpha tester will be the first
person to do it. Please don't let that person be you, mid-service, with a
band on stage.

Start on the demo console. Then an empty scene. Then a rehearsal. Then, if
it's earned it, a service.

When something goes wrong, tell me. **Settings → Help → Email us** fills in
your version, your system and what console you're connected to, so a bug
report costs you one sentence.

## What it costs

Nothing during the alpha. Subscriptions run in Apple's sandbox, so the
paywall works but no money moves.

## What happens to your data

Your mixes stay in a folder you choose — on your own machine, or in your own
Dropbox or iCloud Drive. Nothing is uploaded to me. The [privacy
policy](privacy) is short and says the same thing at more length.
